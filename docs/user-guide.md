# Cortex AI - SaaS Platform User Guide

**Author:** Customer Success & UX Team  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Target:** Staging & Production Beta Pioneers  

---

## 1. Getting Started: Onboarding & Login
To access Cortex AI during our Private Beta, you must possess a valid **Beta Access Code**:
1. Navigate to `https://cortexai.com/register` and enter your name, email, and strong password.
2. Enter your unique **Beta Access Code** (e.g. `CORTEX_BETA_2026`).
3. Complete your **Profile Setup** (setting your full name, preferred locale/language, and timezone).
4. Launch the **Workspace Wizard** to initialize your default Organization workspace.

---

## 2. Navigating the Workspace

### 2.1 The Main Dashboard
Your central dashboard aggregates critical info:
- **Today's Focus:** Highlights your top 3 prioritized tasks for the day.
- **AI Daily Plan:** Displays optimized schedule blocks calculated by the AI.
- **Productivity Score:** Visualizes your completion trends.

### 2.2 Smart Task Workspace
Track, schedule, and execute your tasks through four interactive views:
- **List View:** Table rows with rapid toggle checks.
- **Kanban View:** Drag-and-drop cards between `INBOX`, `PLANNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, and `COMPLETED` columns.
- **Calendar View:** Maps tasks onto scheduled day grids.
- **Timeline View:** Horizontal Gantt timelines tracking parallel projects.

### 2.3 Conversational AI Assistant
Click **AI Assistant** in the sidebar to open the chat pane:
- Type natural prompts like: `"Breakdown my Audit task into checklist steps."`
- Use the quick panels on the right to:
  - **Analyze Tasks:** Force AI Prioritizer to order your queue.
  - **Generate Schedule:** Re-evaluate and timeslot calendar tasks.
  - **Ask Coach:** Get productivity advice based on your completion behaviors.
